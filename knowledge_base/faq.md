## Frequently asked questions

**How do I import existing leads?**
Contacts and deals can be imported via CSV, Excel (.xlsx), or direct migration from HubSpot, Zoho CRM, and Google Sheets in under 5 minutes.

**Does Professional support automation and reports?**
Yes, Professional supports lead automation, automated follow-up sequences, and custom sales reports.

**Can we switch plans or add seats later?**
Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or add extra seats anytime from workspace billing.

**Is my customer data secure?**
All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256), with daily automated backups and role-based permissions.

**क्या पुराने कॉन्टैक्ट्स इम्पोर्ट कर सकते हैं?**
हाँ, आप CSV, Excel या Google Sheets से अपने कॉन्टैक्ट्स आसानी से इम्पोर्ट कर सकते हैं। प्रोफेशनल प्लान में लीड ऑटोमेशन और सेल्स रिपोर्ट्स उपलब्ध हैं।

# Policies

A 14-day free trial is available without a credit card on both Starter and Professional plans. Monthly subscriptions can be canceled at any time and remain active until the end of the current billing period.

Live customer support is available Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST via email, chat, and scheduled video calls. Enterprise customers receive 24/7 priority support and a dedicated onboarding specialist.

# FlowCRM pricing

Starter costs ₹999 per month for up to 5 seats. Professional costs ₹2,999 per month for up to 25 seats and includes automation and analytics. Enterprise pricing is quoted after discovery. No setup fee applies to Starter or Professional.

Annual billing saves 20% on both Starter (₹799/month billed annually) and Professional (₹2,399/month billed annually). Non-profits and early-stage startups under 1 year old are eligible for a 15% discount on Professional.

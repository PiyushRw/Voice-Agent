# Integrations

FlowCRM connects with the tools your sales team already uses:
- **Email & Calendar:** Two-way sync with Gmail, Google Calendar, and Microsoft Outlook / Office 365.
- **Messaging:** WhatsApp Business notifications and Slack deal-stage alerts.
- **Lead Capture:** Web form embeds, webhook API, and Zapier / Make connectors.
- **Telephony & Voice:** Click-to-call logging, call recording transcripts, and automated call summaries.

FlowCRM Gmail, Google Calendar, Outlook, WhatsApp Business और Slack के साथ आसानी से जुड़ता है।

# Products

Starter includes contact management, lead tracking, task reminders, and a visual Kanban deal pipeline for up to five seats.

Professional adds email automation, Gmail and Outlook two-way sync, WhatsApp follow-up templates, custom sales analytics dashboards, and role-based access control for up to 25 seats.

Enterprise offers unlimited seats, single sign-on (SSO / SAML), custom API webhooks, dedicated account manager, SLA guarantees, and guided onboarding support.

## Core Capabilities
- **Contact & Lead Management:** Unified timeline of calls, emails, notes, and deal stages.
- **Automation Workflows:** Auto-assign leads, trigger follow-up emails, and schedule reminders when deals move stages.
- **Analytics & Forecasting:** Conversion rates, pipeline velocity, team leaderboard, and objection trends.
- **Mobile & Web Access:** Full access on desktop browsers, iOS, and Android.

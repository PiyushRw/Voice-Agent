# Company

FlowCRM is a fictional CRM company built for small and growing sales teams in India and globally. Founded in 2022 in Bengaluru, FlowCRM helps teams manage contacts, automate follow-ups, track deal pipelines, and convert leads faster without spreadsheet chaos.

Over 1,200 teams use FlowCRM across SaaS, agencies, real estate, education, and B2B services. Average customers cut manual data entry by 60% and improve follow-up response times by 3x.

FlowCRM एक आधुनिक CRM प्लेटफ़ॉर्म है जो छोटी और बढ़ती सेल्स टीमों को लीड्स, कॉन्टैक्ट्स, फॉलो-अप और डील पाइपलाइन को आसानी से मैनेज करने में मदद करता है।
